What Lookout is

A risky payment waits until someone you trust agrees.

The crew in that reel is every scam call and text that goes after a family's savings. Lookout gives the money a second phone to answer to, so the vault never opens on pressure alone.

Your crew

Six faces keep the vault. You are the Keeper. The person you trust is the Lookout.

The KeeperEl Guardián · Rosa

Holds the wallet and sends the payment. Can cancel a hold — but cannot release one alone.

The LookoutEl Vigía · Maya

How a payment is decided

No single red flag can hold your money, and no secret code ever leaves your phone.

  1. Read the wordsGemini — or the offline rules when it is away — reads the text, the photo, or what you said aloud.
  2. Score the heatA fixed score from 0 to 100. One signal can never hold a payment by itself; the con has to light up more than once.
  3. Rank the whyReasons are ranked by threat. A denial raises a reason, a finished payment lowers it — so the alarm keeps learning.
  4. Redact, then askCodes and card numbers are stripped out before anyone else sees them. Then the hold goes to your Lookout.
